# Diário de Kali

`caderno-kali.png` foi gerado com a habilidade imagegen e a ferramenta integrada em 09/10/2026. Tem 1536 × 1024 pixels, canal de transparência e duas páginas sem texto. O arquivo final está no projeto; o jogo não depende do diretório de geração.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background artwork for an in-game handwritten journal UI, not a mockup. Create an illustration of an old personal notebook opened flat, viewed exactly from above with no perspective tilt, both full pages equally large and rectangular. Dark worn charcoal-brown cloth cover subtly visible around the edges, worn central spine, pale warm ivory yellowed paper with a few small stains and folded corners. Restrained hand-painted illustration appropriate for the somber orphanage OldHood in a narrative mystery game. Concentrate all weathering at outer margins; keep the inner 80% of each page nearly uniform light cream, very low texture, with generous usable empty space for readable dynamic text. The central crease is narrow. Whole notebook fully visible with minimal outer padding, horizontal 3:2 composition. Isolated object on genuinely transparent background. No words, no letters, no scribbles, no religious symbols, no UI elements or buttons, no hands, no desk, no additional objects, no watermark. The final image is just the old open notebook; real text will be placed over it by code.

## Uso

O texto é HTML sobre a imagem. Kalam Regular e Bold, com licença OFL, estão em `assets/fontes/`. No computador, há duas páginas; no celular, o fundo aproveita a página direita. Uma falha da imagem usa papel claro como alternativa. Os diálogos continuam em Solway.
